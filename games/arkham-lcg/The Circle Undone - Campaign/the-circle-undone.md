# The Circle Undone

> *"We are but temporary vessels for forces beyond our comprehension."*

**Cycle:** 4th (2019)
**Format:** Old format — Deluxe (AHC29) + 6 Mythos Packs (AHC30-35)
**Status:** ✅ Owned — not yet played

---

## Overview

The Circle Undone plunges investigators into the shadowy supernatural underbelly of Arkham, where secret societies, witches' covens, and occult conspiracies vie for power. At the heart of it all lies the **Silver Twilight Lodge**, an enigmatic organization whose true purpose is far more sinister than its genteel exterior suggests.

The campaign explores themes of witchcraft, diabolism, and the price of forbidden knowledge, drawing heavily from Lovecraft's "The Case of Charles Dexter Ward" and the Salem witch trials.

## Mechanics

- **Spectral encounter deck** — Scenarios 4+ split the encounter deck into two halves; investigators draw from one or both depending on location/choices
- **Silver Twilight standing** — Your reputation with the Lodge affects scenario events and resolution
- **Protected/hidden cards** — Some locations/cards are hidden behind conditions that must be met
- **Doom threshold manipulation** — Several scenarios involve accelerating or delaying the doom clock

---

## Difficulty

**Hard** — Frequently cited as one of the most difficult campaigns, especially for first-time players. The spectral encounter deck mechanic can create brutal combinations, and some scenarios have demanding time pressure. Recommend Normal difficulty or lower for first play.
