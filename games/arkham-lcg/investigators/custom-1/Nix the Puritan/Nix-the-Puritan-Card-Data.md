
### Elder Sign
```
<eld> +2. Gain 3 resources.
```

### Flavor (front)
```
"I spoke to the Dark, and
the Dark answered me."
```

### Story (back)
```
"And the fire said to me, said to
me... Nix, Nix, you are my instrument.
From now on you shall be known as
'The Puritan'."

Now he walks Arkham's streets and
sees what others cannot. The cultists
with their pathetic rituals, begging
for scraps from an old one that does
not know they exist. The creatures
that serve it; mindless, expendable,
crowding his city like vermin. They
think they own the Dark. They think
they have a claim.

He was there when it spoke.
There is only room for one.
```

### Deckbuilding
```
Deck size: 30.
Mystic cards (level 0-5).
Neutral cards (level 0-5).
Up to 5 level 0 cards from any
other class.
```
