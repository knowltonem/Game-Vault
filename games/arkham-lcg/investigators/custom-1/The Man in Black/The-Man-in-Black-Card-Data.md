### Ability
At the start of the round: You may spend any number of resources. Choose 1 skill (WIL, INT, COM, or AGI). For each 2 resources spent, get +1 to that skill until the end of the round.

### Elder Sign
+1. Gain 3 resources.

### Story
Born in Arkham. Raised in its alleys. He never left because he never needed to.

The underground was his from the start — the debts, the deals, the contacts, the cash. He built it quietly and he runs it the same way. Nobody asks his name. Nobody needs to.

But something has changed in Arkham. The things coming through now — they don't respond to cash. They don't honour debts. They don't understand the rules.

They are bad for business.

He doesn't know what they are. He doesn't care. They're disrupting his city, his streets, his people.

Not on his watch.

### Flavor
*"Cash in the bag. No questions. That's the deal."*

### Requirements
- The Fixer ×1 (Permanent)
- Cash in the Bag ×1 (Permanent)
- Sneaky Pete ×1 (Weakness)
- 1 random basic weakness

## Design Notes

- **Economy identity** — Cash in the Bag generates 1r/turn passively. Ability spends resources for stat boosts. Cash Flow and The Sure Thing provide burst economy. Self-funding loop.
- **Fighter not Seeker** — COM 4 + ability = COM 6-8. INT 3 is his ceiling. Needs a clue-finding partner.
- **2 Ally slots** — Big Tommy (HP soak) + Old Man Winters (HP+SAN soak) both in play simultaneously. Full protection network.
- **The Fixer** — Permanent shotgun. 3 damage + 2r on kill. Best single-target damage in the set.
- **Lights Out** — AoE 2 damage to all enemies, takes 1 horror. Clears swarms.
- **Casing the Joint** — AGI 4 investigation. His only clue engine. Discovers 2 clues on success.
- **Arkham Underground ×2** — Full reload any asset uses. Keeps Saturday Night Special and The Fixer functional all scenario.
