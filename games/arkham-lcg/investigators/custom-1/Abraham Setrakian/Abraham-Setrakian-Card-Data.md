### Ability
```
<rea> Once per round: You may evade
an enemy at your location. This
does not provoke attacks of
opportunity.
```

### Elder Sign
```
<eld> +1. Heal 1 damage and 1 horror
from yourself.
```

### Flavor (front)
```
"They are older than you know.
So am I."
```

### Story (back)
```
I am old. Once I was young and these
things destroyed my village near the
mountains. I fought them then. I
fight them now.

I use them to stave off death — but
at a price. I just hope it is not
my soul.

For now, I hunt them.
```

### Deckbuilding
```
Deck size: 30.
Seeker cards (level 0-5).
Guardian cards (level 0-5).
Neutral cards (level 0-5).
```

### Deckbuilding Restrictions
```
Caput Lupinum begins the game
in play. It is permanent.
```

### Deckbuilding Requirements
```
(do not count toward deck size)
Occido Lumen (2), Thomas Eichhorst,
1 random Basic Weakness.
```
