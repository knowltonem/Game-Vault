## Investigator Card

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Dick Hallorann |
| **Subtitle** | He Knows Things |
| **Class** | Mystic |
| **Traits** | Psychic. Wayfarer. |
| **Willpower** | 4 |
| **Intellect** | 3 |
| **Combat** | 3 |
| **Agility** | 3 |
| **Health** | 7 |
| **Sanity** | 8 |

### Ability
```
<fre> Once per round: Look at the top 2 cards of the encounter deck.
Place them back in any order, or place one on top and one on the bottom.
```

### Elder Sign
```
<eld> +2. Look at the top 3 cards of the encounter deck and arrange them in any order.
```

### Flavor (front)
```
"He knows things. He's always known things."
```

### Story (back)
```
"Dick Hallorann has worked kitchens his whole life.
He's also seen things his whole life — things other
people can't see. He calls it the Shine. It brought
him to Arkham. He doesn't know why yet. He will."
```

### Deckbuilding
```
Deck size: 30.
Mystic cards (level 0-5).
Survivor cards (level 0-5).
Neutral cards (level 0-5).
No Weapon cards.
```
