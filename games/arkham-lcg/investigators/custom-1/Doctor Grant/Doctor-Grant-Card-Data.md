### Ability
```
<fre> When you discover a clue at your location:
You may deal 1 damage to an enemy at your location.
```

### Elder Sign
```
<eld> +2. Gain 1 clue and deal 2 damage to each
enemy at your location.
```

### Auto-fail
```
<aut> 0. Gain 3 resources and deal 1 damage to each
enemy at your location.
```

### Flavor (front)
```
"He stopped looking for dinosaurs the day
he found something older."
```

### Story (back)
```
"Allen Grant spent twenty years digging up
the ancient dead. He was good at it. Then,
in a dig site outside Dunwich, he found
something that predated everything he knew.
Now he's in Arkham, and the things he used
to dig up are the least frightening things
he has ever seen."
```

### Deckbuilding
```
Deck size: 30.
Guardian cards (level 0-5).
Seeker cards (level 0-3).
Neutral cards (level 0-5).
```
