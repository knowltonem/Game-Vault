## Background
Argentine rancher and tracker. Miguel takes an extra action each turn that may only be used to play an event, rewarding event-heavy builds. His signature knapsack lets him play events at a connecting location or draw a card, and his deck is loaded with Trap/Trick attachments. His weakness, Feline Hybrid, is a recurring hunter that is immune to player card effects.
