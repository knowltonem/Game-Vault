## Background
A mechanic whose new garage was literally sunk when the cataclysmic flood devastated Arkham. Now she tunes engines at the meat-packing factories while rumors spread of locals going missing and a shadowy figure stalking the waterfront district. The Chapter 2 core's designated frontline fighter: combat 5 and health 9 let her stand in harm's way, and her reaction fires off a free fight whenever an enemy attacks any investigator at her location. With only 5 sanity and 2 intellect, she leans on her Survivor splash cards for clues and soak.
