## Background
A former spy for the Black Chamber, framed for leaking sensitive information after she uncovered evidence of corruption reaching the top of the organization — rumors of a shadowy group called the Red Coterie. Now on the run, she is driven to restore her good name. She is the core set's evader: a free bonus evade action every turn plus an evasion-focused Rogue kit, with 4 intellect making her a strong secondary clue-finder. Her signature weakness Black Chamber Operative is an enemy she cannot easily shake alone.
