## Elder Sign
> <eld> +1. Heal 1 horror from an investigator at your location.

## Background
Psychologist and researcher. Carolyn heals horror from investigators and Ally assets to discover clues. Her high intellect (5) and tome-based card pool make her a powerful clue-gatherer and support investigator, pairing well with fragile teammates.
