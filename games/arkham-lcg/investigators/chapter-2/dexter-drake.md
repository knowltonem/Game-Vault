## Ability
> <rea> After you play an asset: Either return another non-story asset you control to your hand, or play a different asset from your hand *(paying its cost)*. (Limit once per round.)

## Elder Sign
> <eld> +1. You may return a non-story asset at your location to its owner's hand.

## Background
Stage magician who fought in the Great War and has spent the years since hunting down and translating the pages of the Necronomicon, tempted by the promise of 'true' magic. He is the core set's spellcaster: 5 willpower is the highest stat in the set, and his reaction lets him bounce or chain-play assets to keep his tools in hand and his board fresh. His signature weakness, The Necronomicon (Drake Translation), shuts down his play area until he passes a willpower (5) test.
