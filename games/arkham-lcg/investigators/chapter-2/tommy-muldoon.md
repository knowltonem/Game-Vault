## Ability
> <rea> Spend 1 resource, or discard a [[Firearm]] asset you control from play: Play a [[Firearm]] asset from your hand, paying its cost. It enters play with 2 additional ammo on it.

## Elder Sign
> <eld> +1. Place 1 ammo on an asset you control with uses (ammo).

## Background
Arkham police officer. Tommy's ability keeps him armed to the teeth — spend a resource or discard a Firearm asset from play to play another from hand with 2 extra ammo. His signature, Becky, is a custom Marlin Model 1894 that hits hard and ignores aloof/retaliate. The deck is built around cycling Firearm assets, upgrades, and ammo support.
