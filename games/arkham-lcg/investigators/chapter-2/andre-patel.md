## Background
Film star and charmer. André is a Rogue who earns an additional action whenever he oversucceeds a skill test by 2 or more during his turn. His three signature skills — Know the Exit, Know the Line, Know the Scene — each reward committing to the right type of test, and his weakness, Weight of the World, punishes failure by stacking horror.
