## Background
Private investigator with more experience of supernatural nonsense than most, now swamped with photos of grisly murders and reports of disappearances. He is the core set's beginner-friendly clue engine: every successful investigation draws a card (once per round), keeping him consistent and easy to pilot. Balanced 7 HP / 7 SAN with 4 intellect and 4 combat make him the set's most forgiving all-rounder, at the cost of 2s in willpower and agility.
