## Background
Jazz singer and witch. Marie draws on her Grand-Mère's dark legacy of blood magic — after damage is placed on her, she draws 2 cards and discards 1 of them (Limit once per phase), turning pain into card advantage. Her deck leans into damage-for-effect costs, charges, and curse-charm synergies.

- Neutral cards level 0–5
