## Background
Carson Sinclair has served three generations of the Webb family in Arkham. Always proper, he watched with disapproval as his most recent employer, Hercule Webb, began bringing bizarre artifacts and profane tomes into the house — until Mr. Webb was swallowed by a dimensional rift. No one believed Carson's account, so he has devoted himself to proving the guilt of Webb's business manager, Dupuis, and to restoring the children as rightful heirs to the Webb fortune. Carson is the ultimate support investigator: his extra action can only be spent to hand an action to another investigator, and his signature skill "As you wish" commits to their skill tests instead of his own. His weakness punishes him for ever failing to pitch in.
