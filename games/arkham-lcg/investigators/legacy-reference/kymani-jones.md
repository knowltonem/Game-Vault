## Background
A security consultant whose every job seems to end with the vault cracked and the evidence gone, Kymani Jones dismantles enemies rather than fighting them. Exhaust an enemy (or find one already tired) and Kymani's evasion lets them roll intellect into the check — and if they succeed by the enemy's remaining health, that enemy is discarded outright. Their Grappling Hook strings together three basic actions per activation without provoking attacks of opportunity, and their elder sign turns any exhausted enemy into an automatic success. With 5 agility and 8 health, Kymani is a slippery, disruption-heavy Rogue who begins each campaign with 5 bonus experience.
