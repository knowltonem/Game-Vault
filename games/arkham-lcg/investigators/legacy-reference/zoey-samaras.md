## Background
A chef who hears the voice of God. Raised from the ashes of the house fire that took her parents, Zoey believes she was chosen to protect the innocent and punish the wicked, and she now stalks the night guided by His voice. Her reaction rewards her for drawing enemy attention — she gains 1 resource every time an enemy becomes engaged with her, and those resources fuel her relic cross and her Guardian toolkit. With 4 willpower, 4 combat and a chunky 9 health, she is one of the most durable fighters in the Dunwich cycle. Her signature weakness forces a monster to spawn at the far end of the map as a personal task she must see destroyed before the game ends.
