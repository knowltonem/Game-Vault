## Background
A Nigerian shaman taught by her village's Dibia to commune with the spirits, and marked by them for greatness. Akachi is the queen of charge-based assets: everything with "uses (charges)" enters play with +1 charge, and her elder sign tops off a charge mid-test. Her signature Spirit-Speaker recycles those assets for hand advantage or converts leftover charges into resources. Mystic 0–5 with the unusual option to take any card with "uses (charges)" (0–4) or [[Occult]] (0) — a deck focused entirely on charges.

## Deckbuilding
