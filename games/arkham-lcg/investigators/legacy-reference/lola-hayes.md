## Background
A world-famous actress haunted by The King in Yellow — after her co-star Miriam floated dead in the Seine, she came home to Arkham for one final show. Lola is the game's most unusual investigator: on the first turn she must pick a role, and for the rest of the round she may only play, commit, or trigger abilities on Neutral cards and cards of that role. Her flexible stat line (3/3/3/3) is balanced by that restriction; her elder sign lets her swap roles for free. Deckbuilding is equally exotic — any mix of Survivor, Guardian, Seeker, Rogue, and Mystic (0–3) plus Neutral (0–5), with an additional requirement forcing at least 7 cards each from 3 different classes. Her signature cards lean into the role-shuffling theme: Improvisation switches roles cheaply, and Crisis of Identity shatters whatever role she's in.

## Deckbuilding
- **Deck Size**: 35
- **Deckbuilding Options**: Survivor, Guardian, Seeker, Rogue, and Mystic cards (<survivor>, <guardian>, <seeker>, <rogue>, and <mystic>) level 0–3; Neutral cards level 0–5.
- **Deckbuilding Requirements** (do not count toward deck size): 2 copies of Improvisation, 2 copies of Crisis of Identity, 1 random basic weakness.
- **Additional Requirements**: Your deck must include at least 7 cards each from 3 different classes (<survivor>, <guardian>, <seeker>, <rogue>, or <mystic>).

## Deckbuilding Summary
- **No official starter deck** (Path to Carcosa has no prebuilt deck).
- **Required (not in 35)**: Improvisation ×2, Crisis of Identity ×2, + 1 random basic weakness.
- **Special rules** (from ArkhamDB deck_requirements): deck size 35; Survivor/Guardian/Seeker/Rogue/Mystic level 0–3 + Neutral level 0–5; at least 7 cards each from 3 different classes. ArkhamDB also lists alternate parallel signatures — Leading Lady (90092) and Samuel Blake (90093) from *Enthralling Encore* — as optional replacements for Improvisation and Crisis of Identity.
