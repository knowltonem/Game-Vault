## Background
A reporter for the Arkham Advertiser, Darrell Simmons photographs the horrors no one wants printed. He starts every game with his signature camera, Darrell's Kodak, already in play — it puts evidence tokens on enemies and treacheries as they appear, and moves that evidence onto itself every time he discovers clues. Evidence becomes fuel: each token spent drops the difficulty of a skill test at his location by 2. With 5 intellect he is a top-tier clue-gatherer, and his Survivor flexibility lets him ignore bad luck almost entirely — until Ruined Film devours his hard-won evidence.
