## Elder Sign
> <eld> +1. You may choose a skill card committed to this skill test to return to its owner's hand after this test ends.

## Background
A hard-working secretary whose boss, Mr. Thomas, died by his own hand after reading The King in Yellow — and the play is the key to the truth. Minh is the ultimate team player: every committed card at her location gets a free <wld> icon (once per investigator per round), and her elder sign can recycle a committed skill back to its owner's hand. Her signature asset Analytical Mind lets her commit to tests at other locations too. Seeker 0–5 with a Survivor splash (0–2) makes her a flexible clue/commit hybrid.
