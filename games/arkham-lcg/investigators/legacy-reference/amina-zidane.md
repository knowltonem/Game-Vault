## Elder Sign
> <eld> +2. You may move all doom from a card at your location to another card at your location.

## Background
A radio operator for the mysterious Red Coterie, Amina Zidane carries a curse she was chosen to bear. Her reaction discounts every asset she plays by 3 resources — but each discounted asset enters play bearing 1 doom, so she must manage the doom economy constantly. Her elder sign lets her shuffle doom between cards, and her twin signature events, Word of Woe and Word of Weal, trade doom placement for free asset activations and boosted skill tests, recycling each other from the discard pile. With 9 sanity and even 3s across the board, Amina is a doom-wrangling Mystic who rides the edge between power and the agenda's advance.
