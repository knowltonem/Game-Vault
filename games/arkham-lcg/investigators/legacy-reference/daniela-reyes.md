## Background
Daniela Reyes grew up in her father's Chicago garage and inherited the family shop after mob enforcers killed him. Seeking a fresh start, she signed on as mechanic for the Fate's Gate expedition, keeping the party's vehicles and equipment running through the Antarctic wastes. Her Guardian ability turns being attacked into a weapon: when an enemy strikes her, she either deals it 1 damage or automatically evades it. With 5 Combat — the highest in the cycle — she is a natural brawler, but her build was balanced around the classic Edge of the Earth two-class splash: Guardian level 0 as her foundation, a Survivor splash up to level 5, plus up to 5 level-0 Survivor cards.
