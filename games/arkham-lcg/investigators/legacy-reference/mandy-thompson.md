## Background
A brilliant but anxious assistant researcher at Miskatonic University who lives in the library stacks. Mandy is the ultimate search-engine seeker: whenever anyone at her location searches their deck or the encounter deck, she lets them dig 3 cards deeper or hit one extra target of the search, once per round. Her signature Occult Evidence is shuffled straight into her deck and pops out every time she searches — drawing cards and banking clues. Shocking Discovery is the cruel counterweight, a weakness that cancels searches outright. She can also run a 40- or 50-card deck for extra redundancy, plus a splash of level 0–1 events and skills from other classes.
