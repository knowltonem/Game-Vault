## Background
Lily Chen is a martial artist and mystic, raised by the Order of the Silver Twilight as a "Chosen" ward against evil. Her signature mechanic is her four Disciplines — permanent two-sided assets that begin the game in play on their [[Unbroken]] side, granting a +1 stat boost plus a powerful activated ability that flips them over to their [[Broken]] side; each Broken face then restores itself at round's end if she meets its condition. Her Elder Sign flips a Broken Discipline back to Unbroken, and her signature weakness Burden of Destiny forces her to either break a Discipline (locked for the round) or eat 1 damage and 1 horror. She may not include [[Firearm]] cards, pairing Mystic level 0 with a Guardian splash up to level 5 and up to 5 level-0 Guardian cards. The four Disciplines count toward her 30-card deck, leaving 26 cards for everything else.
