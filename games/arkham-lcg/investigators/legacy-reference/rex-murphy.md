## Background
An investigative reporter whose curiosity has put him face to face with things humanity was not meant to know. His intellect of 4 is among the strongest in the Dunwich cycle, and his ability pays off clean, decisive investigations: every time he succeeds at an investigate test by 2 or more, he discovers an extra clue for free. His signature event, Search for the Truth, converts the clues stacked on him into card draw. His weakness, Rex's Curse, preys on his reliability — it can force him to re-draw a chaos token after a success. His reporter's ethics also restrict his deck: no [[Fortune]] cards.

## Deckbuilding
- Deck size 30.
