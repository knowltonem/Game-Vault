## Background
A dilettante heiress who found more adventure than she bargained for when her sister vanished. Jenny is the classic all-rounder — a 3 in every stat, with nothing below the curve — and her wealth is genuinely useful: she collects an extra resource every upkeep, letting her afford pricey Rogue toys and splashy off-class level 0 cards. Her twin .45s come into play with ammo equal to her current resources, and every upkeep that number grows. Her personal task, Searching for Izzie, forces her to find her lost sister before the game ends or take mental trauma.
