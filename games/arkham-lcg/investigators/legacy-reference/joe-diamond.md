## Ability
> **Forced** - When the investigation phase begins: Reveal the top card of your hunch deck. Until the end of the phase, you may play that card as if it were in your hand, at -2 cost. If it is still revealed at the end of the phase, shuffle it back into your hunch deck.

## Elder Sign
> <eld> +1. You may move an [[Insight]] event from your discard pile to the bottom of your hunch deck.

## Background
Joe Diamond is a private eye with a reputation for handling cases that everyone else regards as supernatural nonsense. He is thorough in his investigations and not one to be trifled with. His services have been employed by the wealthy as well as those down on their luck. No case is too large, too small, too strange, or too dangerous. In Joe's experience, things start getting nasty right when you start reaching the truth, and that is exactly where he likes to be. His oversized 40-card deck pairs solid 4/4 Intellect and Combat stats with a separate "hunch deck" of [[Insight]] events, letting him play the top hunch at -2 cost each investigation phase — an engine that rewards loading up on Insight events.

## Deckbuilding
- Seeker cards level 0–5
- Neutral cards level 0–5
- Guardian cards level 0–2
- **Additional requirement**: Your deck must include at least 11 [[Insight]] events (including Unsolved Case). During the setup of each scenario, you must choose 11 [[Insight]] events from your deck (one of which must be Unsolved Case) and shuffle them into a separate "hunch deck".

**Deckbuilding Requirements** (do not count toward deck size): Detective's Colt 1911s ×1, Unsolved Case ×1, 1 random basic weakness.
