## Ability
> <rea> At the end of your turn, if you started this round 1 location away from your current location: Either gain 1 resource or draw 1 card (if you started this round 2 or more locations away, do both instead).

## Elder Sign
> <eld> +1. If you started this round 1 or more locations away from your current location, gain 1 resource or draw 1 card.

## Background
Monterey Jack is a swashbuckling relic hunter and archaeologist whose love of danger is matched only by his love of ancient treasures. His Rogue ability rewards constant movement: end your turn a location away from where you started and you gain a resource or a card, both if you made a longer trek. Combined with the highest Agility in the cycle, his signature Trusty Bullwhip lets him fight with Agility and either evade the target or hit harder on a success. His build pairs Rogue level 0 with a Seeker splash up to level 5 and up to 5 level-0 Seeker cards.
