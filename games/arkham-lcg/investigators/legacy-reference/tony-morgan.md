## Background
A professional monster hunter for hire who tracks his prey through the Dreamlands. Tony's whole engine runs on bounties: his permanent Bounty Contracts asset stocks 6 bounties and drops them onto every enemy that enters play, and whenever he drops a wanted mark, the bounty converts straight into cold, hard resources. His signature gives him a bonus fight action against any bountied enemy — and an extra action is exactly what a combat 5 rogue wants. Tony's .38 Long Colt rewards stacking bounties on a single enemy for enormous attack bonuses, and Tony's Quarry is a signature enemy weakness that arrives pre-bountied, taunting him from the farthest location.
