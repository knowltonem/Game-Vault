## Background
Norman Withers is a Miskatonic University professor of astronomy whose legitimate discoveries keep being scooped by a mysterious rival, yet who still answers the call to the frozen south. His signature Seeker mechanic runs the game with his deck's top card face-up: he may play it at a discount once per round, swap it out via his Elder Sign, and feed it to skill tests with his tome. The hitch is his Forced effect — a weakness surfacing on top of his deck must be drawn, and his signature weakness The Harbinger can lock his entire deck down until he pays two actions to banish it. His build combines Seeker level 0 with a Mystic splash up to level 5 and up to 5 level-0 Mystic cards. This is the Edge of the Earth (08004) printing; Norman also appears in an earlier promotional printing (98007).
