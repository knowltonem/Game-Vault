## Background
A war-scarred veteran who knows the monsters are real — one killed his beloved Sophie, and he will not rest until every last one is dead. Every wound he takes feeds his hand (draw on damage, limit once per phase), and the more beat-up he gets, the stronger his elder sign becomes. Sophie himself is a strange, double-sided signature: she powers up his tests at the cost of direct damage, then flips into a haunting weakness once he has taken 5+ damage. Guardian staples plus [[Tactic]] cards level 0 round out the deck.
