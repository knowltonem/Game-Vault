## Background
A failed Boston actor who found his true audience as a gravedigger — until he caught degenerate creatures eating the dead, and swore to protect them. Yorick is a recursion machine: every enemy he defeats lets him replay an asset from his discard pile, and his elder sign returns a discard to hand. The graveyard theme runs through his signature cards: Bury Them Deep parks a defeated enemy in the victory display for VPs, while his weakness, Graveyard Ghouls, hunts him and locks his discard pile while engaged. Survivor 0–5 with a Guardian splash (0–2).
