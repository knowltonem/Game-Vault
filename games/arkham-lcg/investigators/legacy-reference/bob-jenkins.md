## Background
Bob Jenkins is a traveling salesman whose "supply chain" philosophy treats the whole party as customers: other investigators may reveal the [[Item]] assets in their hands, and Bob takes an extra action each turn to play one for them — with both players allowed to chip in on the cost. His signature asset Shrewd Dealings discounts every Item he plays and lets him slip them under any investigator's control at his location, while his weakness Greed punishes him for hoarding resources. He is a classic team-support Survivor, pairing Survivor level 0 with a Rogue splash up to level 5 and up to 5 level-0 Rogue cards.
