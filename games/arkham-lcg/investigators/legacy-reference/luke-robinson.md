## Elder Sign
> <eld> +1. Place 1 charge on Gate Box.

## Background
A young dreamer who travels the Dreamlands, never sleeping in the same bed twice. Luke is the most mobile mystic in the game: his Gate Box starts in play, and each turn he can play one event as if standing in any connecting location — so he can sling spells two rooms away or punch through walls with events like [[Open Gate]]. His bonded Dream-Gate location opens a two-way shortcut to any revealed location, fuelled by the elder sign's charge refund. The Detached from Reality weakness drags him into the Pointless Reality side of the Dream-Gate, forcing him to investigate his way out or eat horror. Seekers splash in for clue tools, but the core is spell-events and teleport tricks.
