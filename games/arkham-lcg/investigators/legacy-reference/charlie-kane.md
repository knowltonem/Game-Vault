>
> <fast> During a skill test you are performing, exhaust an [[Ally]] asset you control: For this test, you get +1 skill value, plus an additional +1 for each skill icon that [[Ally]] asset has that matches this test's type.

## Elder Sign
> <eld> +3. Ready an [[Ally]] asset at your location.

## Background
A charismatic politician who surrounds himself with the right people, Charlie Kane commands a retinue instead of a toolbox. With 3 additional ally slots he can field a whole entourage, and during a skill test he exhausts one of his allies to add +1, plus +1 for each matching skill icon on that ally — so the allies he hires determine which tests he can ace. His elder sign is a solid +3 and readies an ally at his location. A true "pick your own class" investigator, Charlie is the only neutral in the set: his deck is built almost entirely around [[Ally]] assets, with a splash of two chosen classes.

## Deckbuilding
- [[Ally]] cards level 0–5.
- Neutral cards level 0–5.
- Class Choices: At deck creation, choose 2 classes (<guardian>, <seeker>, <rogue>, <mystic>, or <survivor>). Cards from your chosen classes level 0–2.
- Deckbuilding requirements (do not count toward deck size): Bonnie Walsh, Burden of Leadership, 1 random basic weakness.
