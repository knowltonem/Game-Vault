## Background
A jazz musician who has glimpsed the "music of the spheres" and plays to keep the darkness in tune. Jim's peculiar talent is statistical: skull tokens count as 0 for him — the game's most common bad draw becomes a neutral — and he may even choose to read an elder sign as a skull when he prefers the predictable result. His trumpet heals horror whenever a skull token is revealed, turning the chaos bag into a healing engine. His weakness, Final Rhapsody, forces him to reveal 5 chaos tokens at once and take damage and horror for every skull and autofail among them.
