## Elder Sign
> <eld> +1. After this test ends, you may shuffle all but 1 card from your discard pile into your deck.

## Background
A gifted violinist whose cursed music has taken her halfway into the Dreamlands — and she may never fully come back. Patrice is the game's boldest deck-engine experiment: she runs a 42-card deck, her hand is capped at 5, and every upkeep she tosses her whole hand into the discard pile and draws back up to five. That churn turns every card in her deck into a tool she will almost certainly see, and her elder sign acts as a discard-pile reset. The Watcher from Another Dimension weakness lives in her hand and can only be fought or evaded from there — brutal in a deck that intentionally runs thin. Survivor resilience plus a splash of Mystic level 0–2 round out her tricks.
