## Ability
> <rea> When an asset you control is defeated: Gain X resources, where X is the total amount of damage and horror on that asset. Shuffle that asset into your deck.

## Elder Sign
> <eld> +2. You may move up to 2 damage and/or horror from Tommy Muldoon to an asset you control, or vice versa.

## Background
A rookie cop who refuses to back down even when his gear gets destroyed. The Dream-Eaters version of Tommy is the ultimate asset-churn guardian: when an asset of his is defeated, he gets paid out for all the damage and horror it soaked, then shuffles it back into his deck to do it all over again. His elder sign lets him shuffle damage and horror off himself onto his own assets — pairing perfectly with that reaction ability. Becky, his signature revolver, turns that resource trickle into ammo, and the Rookie Mistake weakness punishes him for running damaged assets.
