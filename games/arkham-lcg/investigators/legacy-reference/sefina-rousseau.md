## Background
A Tahitian-born painter and master forger whose recreation of a panorama of an alien city sent her hurtling into the painted world. Sefina's signature trick is a rigged opening hand: she draws 13 cards, tucks up to 5 events beneath her, and keeps the best 8 — no mulligan, no wasted draws. The Painted World lets her replay those stashed events on demand. Rogue 0–5 with a Mystic splash (0–2) for spell options, wrapped in a 33-card deck.
