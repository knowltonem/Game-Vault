## Background
A drifter who saw things on the road that he can't explain, and whose loyal dog Duke is the only one who keeps him grounded. Pete begins the game with Duke already in play, and can discard a card from hand once per round to ready any asset he controls — keeping Duke (and his other tools) active turn after turn. Duke attacks with base combat 4 and investigates with base intellect 4, letting Pete act with a strong stat despite his own modest numbers, and his investigate lets him move first. His weakness, Wracked by Nightmares, exhausts every asset he controls and keeps them down until he spends two actions to shake it off.
