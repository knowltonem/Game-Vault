## Background
Not many people can say that they've climbed a mountain on every continent. Fewer still can say they've stolen a golden idol from a tribe of cannibals. Ever since she was a small girl, Ursula has broken the rules set down for her — climbing trees, playing in the mud, exploring the woods — and since earning a degree in archaeology in Boston, she has been traveling the world searching for forgotten civilizations. In gameplay she is the quintessential action-economy Seeker: moving to a new location triggers a free investigate action, and her elder sign lets her slip to a connecting location after a successful test, keeping the party moving at full speed.
