## Background
A dedicated doctor who has seen too many patients carried into his office broken by things that should not exist, Vincent Lee treats the wounds — physical and mental — that the mythos leaves behind. Every time one of his card effects heals damage, he deals out a set-aside On the Mend skill to that investigator's hand, turning healing into recurring card advantage. His signature Bonesaw doubles as a surgeon's tool and a weapon (self-damage buys extra fight), and his Wounded Bystander weakness must be kept alive or he suffers lasting trauma. The rare healer who can both investigate and fight, Vincent leans on Seeker tools plus a splash of Guardian/Survivor support.

## Deckbuilding
- <seeker> Seeker cards level 0–3.
- Neutral cards level 0–5.
- Cards that "heal damage" level 0–5.
- Up to 15 other <guardian> Guardian and/or <survivor> Survivor cards level 0–1.
- Deckbuilding requirements (do not count toward deck size): Bonesaw, X copies of On the Mend (X is the number of investigators, rounded up to the nearest even number), Wounded Bystander, 1 random basic weakness.
- Additional setup: You begin each game with X copies of On the Mend set aside, out of play.
