## Elder Sign
> <eld> +1. After this test ends, for every 2 points you succeeded by, return a card you committed to this test to your hand.

## Background
An aviatrix and thrill-seeker who flies by the seat of her pants, Winifred Habbamock turned to crime to keep her plane — and herself — in the air. With the game's highest Agility among the starter-deck investigators (5), she thrives on big margins of success: her ability draws a card whenever two different non-weakness cards are committed to a test, and her signature skill commits six wild icons to make over-succeeding a habit. The starter deck is built around Trick events (Pilfer, Cheap Shot, Slip Away) and skills that reward success (Daredevil, Opportunist, Nimble).
