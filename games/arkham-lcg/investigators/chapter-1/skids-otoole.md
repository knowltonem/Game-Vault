## Background
Ex-con troubled by the mad ravings of his former cellmate. His ability lets him spend 2 resources to gain an extra action — potent but expensive, requiring strong resource generation to fuel. His flat stat line has no clear standout skill, and he is generally considered the weakest of the core five; his Rogue resource engines and Guardian splash for weapons make him flexible, if demanding to play well.

## Deckbuilding Summary
- **Starting 30**: 25 unique level-0 cards — 10 Rogue, 10 Guardian, 5 Neutral (20 cards ×1, 5 cards ×2: Knife, Flashlight, Emergency Cache, Guts, Overpower)
- **Required (not in 30)**: On the Lam ×1, Hospital Debts ×1, Mob Enforcer ×1 (basic weakness assigned by the Revised Core Learn to Play guide)
- **Upgrades**: No fixed list — the Revised Core Set includes level 1–5 class upgrades (e.g. .41 Derringer (2), Lockpicks (3), Leo De Luca (3)) available via normal deckbuilding rules
- **All starter deck cards are level 0**
