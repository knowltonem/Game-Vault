## Ability
> <rea> When an investigator at your location would reveal any number of chaos tokens: Reveal 2 additional tokens. Of the revealed tokens, choose and cancel 2 non-<auto_fail> tokens, or 1 <auto_fail> token. (Limit once per round.)

## Elder Sign
> <eld> +1. If this effect is canceled or ignored, draw 1 card.

## Background
A famed psychic medium who makes her living giving séances, hiding her true clairvoyance behind a fortune-teller's act. Her Willpower of 5 is among the highest in the game — she sees what fate has in store and simply rewrites it. Her ability cancels the worst of any token reveal at her location, and the starter deck leans hard into chaos-bag control: token-drawing cards like Dark Prophecy, Scrying Mirror, and Grotesque Statue, token-neutralizing skills like Defiance, and the classic spell suite of Azure Flame, Clairvoyance, and Ineffable Truth, which all test with her elite Willpower.
