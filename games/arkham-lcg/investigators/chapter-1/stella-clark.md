## Background
A letter carrier from Arkham whose true name is whispered to her by the house on the cliff in Kingsport. Stella is the ultimate "fail-forward" investigator: every failed skill test grants her an extra action, and her deck is packed with cards that convert failures into value — Live and Learn, "Look what I found!", Oops!, and Rabbit's Foot all trigger off the same misses her ability rewards. Her agility of 4 is her standout stat, with balanced 3s in willpower and combat and a generous 8 health / 8 sanity letting her absorb setbacks.
