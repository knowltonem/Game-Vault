## Background
A hard-hitting boxer who serves as a warden at a juvenile detention center. His trainer-turned-partner, Randall Cho, taught him to channel his anger. His combat stat of 5 is among the highest in the game — he lives to throw punches and back them up with Spirit/Tactic events. The starter deck is heavily event-driven: almost every fight is boosted by his ability (extra damage when events deal damage).
