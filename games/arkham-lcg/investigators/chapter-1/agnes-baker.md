## Background
Waitress who has begun to discover memories of a past life as a powerful sorceress. The highest willpower (5) in the core set — an exceptional spell-caster. When she suffers horror, she deals 1 damage to any enemy at her location, turning her own pain into a weapon. A high-risk, high-reward investigator that plays best in experienced hands; her Survivor splash adds unusual utility and recovery options.

## Deckbuilding Summary
- **Starting 30**: 25 unique level-0 cards — 10 Mystic, 10 Survivor, 5 Neutral (20 cards ×1, 5 cards ×2: Knife, Flashlight, Emergency Cache, Perception, Unexpected Courage)
- **Required (not in 30)**: Heirloom of Hyperborea ×1, Dark Memory ×1, Haunted ×1 (basic weakness assigned by the Revised Core Learn to Play guide)
- **Upgrades**: No fixed list — the Revised Core Set includes level 1–5 class upgrades (e.g. Rite of Seeking (3), Mind Wipe (3)) available via normal deckbuilding rules
- **All starter deck cards are level 0**
