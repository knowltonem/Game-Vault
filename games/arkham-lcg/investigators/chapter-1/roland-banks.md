## Background
FBI agent whose recent cases have led him to conclusions the public isn't ready for. A solid all-rounder — strong combat (4) with enough intellect (3) to gather clues on the side. His ability rewards defeating enemies with free clue discovery, making him a hybrid fighter/investigator who works well both solo and in groups. One of the most accessible investigators in the game, and the reference point against which the others are usually measured; his low sanity (5) and clue-hoarding signature weakness, Cover Up, keep him on a clock.

## Deckbuilding Summary
- **Starting 30**: 25 unique level-0 cards — 10 Guardian, 10 Seeker, 5 Neutral (20 cards ×1, 5 cards ×2: Knife, Flashlight, Emergency Cache, Guts, Manual Dexterity)
- **Required (not in 30)**: Roland's .38 Special ×1, Cover Up ×1, Silver Twilight Acolyte ×1 (basic weakness assigned by the Revised Core Learn to Play guide)
- **Upgrades**: No fixed list — the Revised Core Set includes level 1–5 class upgrades (e.g. Beat Cop (2), Shotgun (4)) available via normal deckbuilding rules
- **All starter deck cards are level 0**
