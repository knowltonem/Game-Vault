## Background
An erudite professor of the occult at Miskatonic University, Harvey is a fount of knowledge on the bizarre primordial religions that worshipped nightmarish gods eons ago. He excels at drawing cards and keeping a hand flush with cards — his 5 Intellect and card-draw ability make him the quintessential big-hand Seeker. The starter deck is built around hand size: keep 10+ cards in hand to trigger Higher Education, Celaeno Fragments, and a cheap Extensive Research, while Vault of Knowledge and Laboratory Assistant expand his maximum hand size. His weakness is enemy management (1 Combat, 2 Agility), so early game he leans on Disc of Itzamna and Occult Invocation until upgrades arrive.
