## Elder Sign
> <eld> +0. If you succeed, draw 1 card for each [[Tome]] you control.

## Background
Librarian with custody over books that perhaps never should have been written. The highest base intellect (5) in the core set — an exceptional clue-gatherer whose Tome ability grants an extra action each turn. Powerful in the right build and widely considered one of the best investigators in the full card pool, she is frail in combat (2) with only 5 health and needs teammates to handle enemies.

## Deckbuilding Summary
- **Starting 30**: 25 unique level-0 cards — 10 Seeker, 10 Mystic, 5 Neutral (20 cards ×1, 5 cards ×2: Knife, Flashlight, Emergency Cache, Perception, Manual Dexterity)
- **Required (not in 30)**: Daisy's Tote Bag ×1, The Necronomicon ×1, Paranoia ×1 (basic weakness assigned by the Revised Core Learn to Play guide)
- **Upgrades**: No fixed list — the Revised Core Set includes level 1–5 class upgrades (e.g. Encyclopedia (2), Cryptic Research (4)) available via normal deckbuilding rules
- **All starter deck cards are level 0**
