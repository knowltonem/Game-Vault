## Elder Sign
> <eld> +0. If Wendy's Amulet is in play, you automatically succeed instead.

## Background
Street urchin with little to her name besides a mysterious family heirloom. Her ability lets her discard the top card of her deck to cancel a chaos token draw and redraw — a powerful safety net, and the reason she is one of the strongest solo investigators in the game. With the core set's best evade value (agility 4) and a very low combat (1), she never fights if she can help it; her Survivor/Rogue card access focuses on evasion, resource generation, and chaos-bag manipulation.

## Deckbuilding Summary
- **Starting 30**: 25 unique level-0 cards — 10 Survivor, 10 Rogue, 5 Neutral (20 cards ×1, 5 cards ×2: Knife, Flashlight, Emergency Cache, Overpower, Unexpected Courage)
- **Required (not in 30)**: Wendy's Amulet ×1, Abandoned and Alone ×1, Amnesia ×1 (basic weakness assigned by the Revised Core Learn to Play guide)
- **Upgrades**: No fixed list — the Revised Core Set includes level 1–5 class upgrades (e.g. Will to Survive (4), Eucatastrophe (5)) available via normal deckbuilding rules
- **All starter deck cards are level 0**
