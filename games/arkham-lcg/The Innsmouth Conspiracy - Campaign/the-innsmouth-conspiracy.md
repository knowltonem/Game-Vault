# The Innsmouth Conspiracy

> *"The sea has its own children, and they remember everything."*

**Cycle:** 6th (2020)
**Format:** Old format — Deluxe (AHC52) + 6 Mythos Packs (AHC53-58)
**Status:** ✅ Owned — not yet played

---

## Overview

The Innsmouth Conspiracy sends investigators to the decaying seaside town of Innsmouth, Massachusetts, where the deep ones walk among men and a sinister conspiracy threatens to rise from the depths. Drawing from Lovecraft's classic *The Shadow over Innsmouth*, the campaign weaves a tale of genetic degeneration, cosmic hybridization, and the terrible truth beneath the waves.

This campaign introduced the **bless and curse token** mechanic to the chaos bag, which became a staple of the game going forward.

## Mechanics

- **Bless/Curse tokens** — Introduced in this cycle. Adds special tokens to the chaos bag that improve (bless) or worsen (curse) your odds. First appearance of this mechanic in the game
- **Flood tokens** — Locations can become partially or fully flooded, changing their properties and movement options
- **Vehicle mechanics** — Scenario 4 uses the *Fishing Vessel* as a mobile base of operations with its own damage/horror track
- **Keys** — Special objective tokens that unlock locations or advance the story
- **Memory mechanic** — Some treacheries and effects interact with the number of "memory" cards you've accumulated

---

## Difficulty

**Medium-Hard** — Combat-heavy campaign with some brutally punishing scenarios (Horror in High Gear and Lair of Dagon are notorious). The bless/curse mechanic adds a new strategic layer. Recommended for players comfortable with combat-focused play.
